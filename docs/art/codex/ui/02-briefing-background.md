## Image

`briefing_station.png`: **1920 × 1080 px**, RGB.

- **Scene:** central Stalingrad around the main railway station, September 1942. The station building and nearby blocks damaged and burning, smoke columns rising, a railway yard with wrecked freight wagons in the foreground, the Volga hazy in the far distance.
- **Viewpoint:** elevated oblique view (as from a high window or a low aircraft), not top-down.
- **Style:** painterly and realistic, muted and desaturated (dusty ochre, grey, brown), soft overcast light with an orange glow from fires. It should feel like a period painting or a colorized photograph, not a modern action poster.
- **Composition:** keep the **center third calm and darker** (the briefing panel covers it); detail toward the edges.
- **No people in close-up**, no readable text, signs or numbers, and **no flags, no swastikas, no red stars, no political symbols** anywhere.
