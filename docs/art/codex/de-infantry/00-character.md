# Character: German rifleman (Wehrmacht infantry, Stalingrad, September 1942)

- **Output folder for every task in this set:** `ArtSource/codex/de_infantry/`
- **Reference image:** `ArtSource/codex/de_infantry/de_rifleman_01_stand.png`
  - It is created by task `01-stand-reference.md`.
  - Every other task in this set must use it as the visual reference.
  - If it does not exist yet, stop and say so. Do not invent a new look.

## Appearance (identical in every frame)

- **Helmet:** M1935 steel helmet (Stahlhelm), painted field grey, with a matte finish. Seen from above it is a smooth dome with a slightly flared rim. No decals are visible from above.
- **Tunic and trousers:** field-grey wool M40 tunic, dusty and worn. Trousers are tucked into black leather jackboots.
- **Webbing:**
  - Black leather Y-straps and belt.
  - Two sets of black ammunition pouches at the front of the belt.
  - Bread bag and canteen at the back right hip.
  - Entrenching tool at the back left hip.
  - Gas mask canister (fluted, grey-green metal cylinder) slung across the back.
- **Weapon:** Kar98k bolt-action rifle, with a wood stock and dark metal. It only needs to read clearly as a rifle silhouette.
- **Build:** average height and build. The proportions stay the same across all frames.
