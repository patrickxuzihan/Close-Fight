# Task R2: roster pictures of the four vehicles

Read `docs/art/codex/roster/00-picture-guide.md` first.

**Output folder:** `ArtSource/codex/roster/`

Four pictures. Each shows the whole vehicle with its hatches closed and **no crew**.

## `unit_stug3f.png`: StuG III Ausf. F (German assault gun, 1942)

Shape reference: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/veh_stug3f_hull.png`.

**Must show:**

- **No turret.**
- A low, wide, box-shaped casemate (a fixed superstructure):
  - it sits on the fenders and covers the front two thirds of the hull;
  - it has flat, sloped side plates.
- The long 7.5 cm StuK 40 L/43 gun:
  - it sits in a big, boxy, slab-sided mantlet in the casemate front, a little right of centre;
  - it ends in a round, single-chamber muzzle brake.
- The Panzer III running gear:
  - 6 small road wheels a side;
  - 3 return rollers;
  - the drive sprocket at the front.
- The commander's hatch with a periscope at the rear left of the casemate roof.
- Dark grey paint.

**Must not:**

- a turret;
- a tall or narrow superstructure;
- a short gun;
- a two-chamber muzzle brake (that is the later L/48);
- side skirts.

## `unit_pz3j.png`: Panzer III Ausf. J (German medium tank, 1942)

Shape reference: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/veh_pz3j_assembly_preview.png`.

**Must show:**

- A boxy hull with vertical sides and fenders over the tracks.
- The turret in the middle of the hull:
  - boxy, with sloped sides;
  - a hatch in each side;
  - the commander's cupola at the rear of the roof;
  - a storage box on the turret rear.
- The long, thin 5 cm KwK 39 L/60 gun:
  - no muzzle brake;
  - it sticks out well beyond the front of the hull.
- 6 small road wheels a side and 3 return rollers.
- Dark grey paint.

**Must not:**

- a short gun;
- a muzzle brake;
- side skirts;
- a turret set off-centre.

## `unit_t34_76.png`: T-34/76, 1942 model (Soviet medium tank)

Shape reference: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/veh_t34_76_hull.png` and `veh_t34_76_turret.png`.

**Must show:**

- A steeply sloped front plate and sloped sides; the hull is low and wide.
- The hexagonal cast turret:
  - set far forward on the hull;
  - two round hatches on its roof.
- The 76.2 mm F-34 gun: medium length, no muzzle brake.
- The running gear:
  - five large road wheels a side;
  - no return rollers;
  - wide tracks with big flat links.
- Protective green paint.

**Must not:**

- the T-34-85's long turret and longer gun;
- small road wheels or return rollers;
- slogans, stars or numbers.

## `unit_t60.png`: T-60 (Soviet light tank, 1942)

Shape reference: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/veh_t60_assembled_preview.png`.

**Must show:**

- A very small, low tank. It is only about 4 m long, but it still fills the frame like the others.
- A small hull with a sloped front.
- A small turret set to the left of the centre line, with a short, thin 20 mm gun.
- The running gear:
  - four road wheels a side;
  - three return rollers;
  - narrow tracks.
- Protective green paint.

**Must not:**

- look like a T-70 (a bigger turret with a 45 mm gun) or a T-26 (two turrets or many small wheels);
- a turret in the middle of the hull.
