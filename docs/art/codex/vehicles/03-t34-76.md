# Task V3: T-34/76 model 1942, Stalingrad Tractor Factory build (Soviet)

Before starting, read `docs/art/codex/00-style-guide.md` and `docs/art/codex/vehicles/00-vehicle-guide.md`.
Output folder: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/`

## What it is

The T-34 built at the Stalingrad Tractor Factory (STZ) in 1942, the tanks that drove straight from the factory into the fighting. It has a sloped hull and wide tracks, and a turret set far forward. The hull and turret are separate images.

## Images

- `veh_t34_76_hull.png`: 512 × 1024 canvas, pointing north, **without the turret**.
  - Hull length: **5.92 m = 758 px**. Width: **3.00 m = 384 px**.
  - The **turret ring opening sits on the center line, 0.90 m (115 px) forward of the hull center**: canvas (256, 397).
  - The front glacis is sloped, with the driver's hatch on the left front and a hull machine gun ball mount on the right front. The rear engine deck is long, with grilles and a big round-cornered transmission access hatch at the very rear, and external fuel tanks on the hull sides.
  - Wide tracks with large road wheels (5 a side) show beside the hull. STZ tanks often had plain, unrubbered road wheels.
- `veh_t34_76_turret.png`: 1024 × 1024 canvas, pointing north.
  - A cast, rounded turret about **2.2 m long × 2.0 m wide** (282 × 256 px), with one large hatch on the turret roof toward the rear, and a cast gun mantlet.
  - **76.2 mm F-34 barrel: 3.1 m (397 px)** from the mantlet front, with no muzzle brake.
  - The **turret ring center** sits at canvas (512, 512).

## Checks

Report the hull length, the turret ring position on the hull and the turret pivot. No crew, no red stars, no slogans, no numbers.
