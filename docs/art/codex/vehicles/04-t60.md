# Task V4: T-60 light tank (Soviet, 1942)

Before starting, read `docs/art/codex/00-style-guide.md` and `docs/art/codex/vehicles/00-vehicle-guide.md`.
Output folder: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/`

## What it is

A small two-man scout tank with a tiny turret offset to the left. The hull and turret are separate images.

## Images

- `veh_t60_hull.png`: 512 × 1024 canvas, pointing north, **without the turret**.
  - Hull length: **4.10 m = 525 px**. Width: **2.30 m = 294 px**.
  - The **turret ring center is 0.35 m (45 px) left of the center line and 0.30 m (38 px) forward of the hull center**: canvas (211, 474).
  - The driver's position is a raised box on the front center. The engine deck is on the right side and rear. The tracks have 4 small road wheels a side.
- `veh_t60_turret.png`: 1024 × 1024 canvas, pointing north.
  - A small, angular, faceted turret about **1.4 m across** (179 px), with one roof hatch.
  - **20 mm TNSh autocannon barrel: 1.6 m (205 px)**, thin.
  - The **turret ring center** sits at canvas (512, 512).
