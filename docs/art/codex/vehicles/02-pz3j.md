# Task V2: Panzer III Ausf. J (long 5 cm gun, German, 1942)

Before starting, read `docs/art/codex/00-style-guide.md` and `docs/art/codex/vehicles/00-vehicle-guide.md`.
Output folder: `ArtSource/codex/vehicles/`

## What it is

A medium tank with a three-man turret. The hull and turret are separate images so that the turret can turn in game.

## Images

- `veh_pz3j_hull.png`: 512 × 1024 canvas, pointing north, **without the turret**.
  - Hull length: **5.56 m = 712 px**. Width over the tracks: **2.95 m = 378 px**.
  - The **turret ring opening is centered on the hull's center line, 0.10 m (13 px) forward of the hull center**: canvas (256, 499).
  - The front hull roof has the driver's and radio operator's hatches. The rear engine deck has two large hatches, grilles and tools along the sides. The tracks have 6 road wheels a side.
- `veh_pz3j_turret.png`: 1024 × 1024 canvas, pointing north.
  - Turret about **1.9 m wide × 2.1 m long** (243 × 269 px), with a flat roof, the commander's cupola at the rear, side hatches visible as outlines, and a storage box on the turret rear.
  - **Long 5 cm KwK 39 L/60 barrel: 3.0 m (384 px)** from the mantlet front, thin, with no muzzle brake.
  - The **turret ring center** sits at canvas (512, 512); the barrel extends north from it.

## Checks

Report the hull length, the turret ring position on the hull and the turret pivot in `notes.md`. No crew and no swastikas.
