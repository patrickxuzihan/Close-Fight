# Character: Soviet rifleman (Red Army infantry, Stalingrad, September 1942)

- **Output folder for every task in this set:** `ArtSource/codex/su_infantry/`
- **Reference image:** `ArtSource/codex/su_infantry/su_rifleman_01_stand.png`
  - It is created by task `01-stand-reference.md`.
  - Every other task in this set must use it as the visual reference.
  - If it does not exist yet, stop and say so. Do not invent a new look.
- **Scale, camera and pivots** follow `docs/art/codex/00-style-guide.md` exactly, as for the German rifleman: 256 × 256 frames, 128 px = 1 m, helmet 34 px wide.

## Appearance (identical in every frame)

- **Helmet:** SSh-40 steel helmet, painted dull olive green, with a matte finish. Seen from above it is a rounder, simpler dome than the German helmet, with a short even rim and no flare. No stars or other markings are visible from above.
- **Uniform:** khaki-brown (faded, dusty) gymnastyorka tunic with a stand-up collar, and matching trousers. Black leather boots, or ankle boots with khaki puttees wound to below the knee.
- **Rolled greatcoat (skatka):** a brown-grey rolled coat worn as a hoop over the left shoulder and across to the right hip. It is very visible from above and the most recognisable Soviet feature, so keep it clear.
- **Webbing:**
  - Brown leather or canvas waist belt with a brass-coloured buckle.
  - Two brown leather ammunition pouches at the front of the belt.
  - A small entrenching tool in a canvas cover at the left hip.
  - A khaki canvas gas mask bag at the left hip.
  - A khaki veshchmeshok (drawstring duffel bag) on the back, with its straps over both shoulders.
- **Weapon:** Mosin-Nagant M1891/30 rifle, with a wood stock and dark metal, and a long spike bayonet fixed. The silhouette is longer than the German rifle.
- **Build:** average height and build, the same proportions as the German rifleman. The proportions stay the same across all frames.
- **Absolutely NO red stars, hammer and sickle, flags or slogans** visible anywhere.
